# Video Thumbnail Generator release notes

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-18)

First release: frames at a timestamp with a scrubbing preview, evenly spaced frames, scene-change picks, contact sheets and animated GIF/WebP previews, with an ffprobe info panel. HDR tone mapping, rotation and deinterlacing applied. Bundles unmodified ffmpeg 9.0.2 (GPL); its sources are attached to the release.
